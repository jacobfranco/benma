Metrics use three human consensus classes. An abstention is a fourth prediction
column and counts as a missed recall opportunity, including on incorrect speech.
Disputed/ungradable/unlabeled rows are retained in coverage and prediction-count
reports but never given fabricated three-class truth labels. Zero denominators
are `null`, not perfect scores. Safety rates include raw numerators/denominators.
Frozen Benma supports are not probabilities; its calibration is not reported.
