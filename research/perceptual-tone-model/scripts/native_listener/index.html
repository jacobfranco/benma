Listening study

Judge the sound of the indicated syllable. Use headphones in a quiet place. Work alone and do not discuss answers. You may stop at any time.

Some trials play one syllable. Others play a short passage, followed by that same target syllable. The shaded timing bar marks the target in the passage. Judge the target's sound, even if you recognize the word. Low or dipping realizations may be Tone 3. Choose Neutral / light tone for a weak unstressed syllable, or Unclear / cannot tell if the sound is clipped, incomplete, or hard to judge.
