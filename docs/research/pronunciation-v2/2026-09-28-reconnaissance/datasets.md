# Mandarin data reconnaissance — 2026-09-28

This is a source audit, not a downloaded-corpus audit. Counts describe published releases, not locally verified usable tone instances. GREEN/YELLOW/RED are defined in [licensing.md](licensing.md). No corpus was downloaded. A transcript, a prompted tone and a pronunciation-correctness score are three different things; none alone certifies an observed tone.

## Learner-specific findings

There **is** a commercially permitted candidate: OMPAL. Its `tone: 1` means correct, **not Tone 1**. Incorrect examples cannot be assigned a realized class by subtracting the expected tone. Audit wrong-tone examples into T1–T4/ambiguous/neutral/segment-mismatch, preserving original correctness scores separately. Reserve the entire corpus from training, tuning and calibration in the first experiment. French-L1 results do not establish performance for English-L1 or all learners. Three native speakers cannot alone support the native benchmark.

LATIC is a relevant small Mandarin-L2 corpus. The primary IEEE DataPort URL was robots-blocked, so neither permission nor release annotations were verified; **YELLOW**, no use inferred from “open access.” A primary MDD study cites it as the available small L2-Mandarin corpus; use that only as evidence of research relevance, not licensing. [Data owner landing page](https://ieee-dataport.org/open-access/latic-non-native-pre-labelled-mandarin-chinese-validation-corpus-automatic-speech), [MDD paper](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2024/wang24la_interspeech.pdf).

The Griffith/Nanjing Australian learner collection includes recordings and TextGrids; its institutional metadata states CC BY-ND 4.0. Commercial use is not categorically banned by ND, but sharing adapted crops/annotations and the proposed ML workflow need review: **YELLOW**, not automatically RED. Speaker count/hours not verified. [Institution-supplied record](https://researchdata.edu.au/audio-recordings-l2-errors-dataset/1465210).

SpeechOcean762 and L2-ARCTIC do not solve Mandarin-L2 evaluation: they contain learners speaking **English**, including Mandarin-L1 speakers. [SpeechOcean authors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.01378). Benma phone recordings remain an untouched product challenge set, never training material.

## Practical acquisition decision

No ready-to-run public native-training plus automatic-alignment chain was verified GREEN end to end under the requested strict policy. This is not a claim that these corpora are unlawful. Resolve a specific acquisition/use question, or use the directly licensed micro-corpus; do not lower the policy because a neural model looks promising.

The most economical public route to clear first is **private commercial training on a small Common Voice subset, with no audio redistribution**, including whether internal crops/caches and sharing classifier weights are permitted. AISHELL-3 is next because its listening-derived pinyin may substantially reduce labeling labor. Neither clearance is assumed here. [Experiment specification](experiment-plan.md) uses fixed counts and manual alignment so an unresolved pretrained aligner does not become a hidden dependency.
