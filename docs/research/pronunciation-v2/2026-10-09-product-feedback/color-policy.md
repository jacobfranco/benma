# Pitch-only color feedback

Color comes from production learner grading and explicit chunk aggregation.
It describes pitch agreement, not consonants, vowels or overall pronunciation.

| Color | Symbol | Accessible meaning | Policy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Green | ✓ | Strong pitch match | Strong compatible family or supported contextual neutral |
| Amber | ≈ | Partial pitch match | Compatible but weak/reduced realization |
| Red | ≠ | Pitch differs | Stable incompatible family |
| Gray | — | Pitch unavailable | Acoustic or linguistic judgment unavailable |

Red means Benma could judge the pitch and it sounded like another family. Gray
means Benma could not judge it. Unresolved surface context alone does not force gray
when a resolved lexical family is available; details distinguish that fallback.

`pitchGradePresentation` is the shared production/audit mapping. Red uses #a32332
ink on #fcecef background (checked in the browser), with the ≠ symbol and accessible
“Pitch differs” text. Overview symbols retain the existing neutral chip backgrounds.
Selected status text uses a light semantic background. No confidence percentages.

More Details shows “Tone N family” with connected-speech coaching for lexical
fallback, explicit surface expectations when resolved, and flexible neutral copy.
Measured neutral constituents show positive contextual acceptance without a numbered
winner label. Unmeasured neutral stays unavailable with non-veto explanation.
Technical details preserve saved strict diagnostics and raw evidence.

The graph size, timing, gaps, local scale and exact playback ownership are unchanged.
Native/web tests and the offline browser cover the four states, expectation details,
neutral acceptance, grouped timing, keyboard/dialog behavior and history.
